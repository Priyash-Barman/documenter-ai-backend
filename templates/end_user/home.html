Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats are supported?

We currently support image formats including JPG, PNG, GIF, and BMP. PDF support is coming soon!

How secure is my data?

Your documents are encrypted during processing and are never stored on our servers. They are automatically deleted after processing for maximum security.

How accurate is the text extraction?

Our AI models achieve over 95% accuracy on standard documents. Accuracy may vary based on image quality, fonts, and document complexity.

Is there a limit to how many documents I can process?

Free accounts can process up to 50 documents per month. Premium accounts have higher limits based on their subscription tier.
